# Comp-Graphic-and-Visualization

1. How do I approach designing software?
What new design skills has your work on the project helped you to craft?
This project improved my ability to break a complex real-world scene into simple geometric shapes and organize objects within a 3D environment using transformations, textures, and lighting.

What design process did you follow for your project work?
I started by studying the reference image, identifying the objects, building them with primitive shapes, applying textures, adding lighting, and then adjusting positions and proportions to match the photograph.

How could tactics from your design approach be applied in future work?
Breaking large problems into smaller tasks and building features incrementally can be used in future software and graphics projects to make development more organized and manageable.

2. How do I approach developing programs?
What new development strategies did you use while working on your 3D scene?
I used incremental development by creating one feature at a time, such as objects, textures, lighting, and camera controls. I also relied on modular functions to keep the code organized.

How did iteration factor into your development?
Iteration was essential because I repeatedly tested and adjusted object placement, materials, textures, and lighting until the scene more closely matched the reference image.

How has your approach to developing code evolved throughout the milestones?
Throughout the milestones, I became more focused on code organization, reusability, and debugging. I learned to separate functionality into reusable functions rather than placing everything in one location.

3. How can computer science help me in reaching my goals?
How do computational graphics and visualizations give you new knowledge and skills that can be applied in your future educational pathway?
This course strengthened my understanding of transformations, lighting, shaders, textures, and 3D visualization. These concepts are valuable for advanced computer science topics and future coursework.

How do computational graphics and visualizations give you new knowledge and skills that can be applied in your future professional pathway?
The project improved my skills in problem-solving, software development, debugging, and visualization. These skills can be applied in careers involving software engineering, simulation, game development, visualization, and user interface design.
